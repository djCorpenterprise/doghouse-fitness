The story

Built by a pitcher,
a golden retriever,
and the work.

Dog House Fitness started in a garage in Julington Creek. A former D1 baseball pitcher, a golden retriever named Gio, and a simple belief that discipline is not optional for anyone who lives in this house.

Gio does not get a rest day. He does not skip the session because it is raining. He shows up every time, ready to work, carrying whatever you put in front of him with complete commitment. That is the standard around here.

The brand grew from a question nobody was asking: why does all dog gear look like it belongs in a big box pet store? Your dog trains with you. Your dog lives by the same code. Your dog deserves gear that looks the part.

Faithful to the work. Every rep. Every day. Every member of the household.
